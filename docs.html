Everything needed to build, flash, wire and integrate Arsin Print Server - on ESP32, ESP8266 and Raspberry Pi.

1. Flash the board

Open the installer, choose an application, a transport and your board. ESP32/ESP8266 flash from the browser with Web Serial; Raspberry Pi uses Raspberry Pi Imager plus a one-line installer.

2. Wire the printer

The installer generates a diagram, a pin table, a bill of materials and a printable guide for your exact board and transport. The wiring library lists every combination.

3. Join it to your network

The board creates the Wi-Fi network arsin_print_server (password arsin1234). The captive portal opens the wizard; enter your office Wi-Fi once and the bridge reconnects forever. Then use http://arsin_print_server.local.

4. Add it to your POS / ERP

Add a network printer on port 9100 (raw / JetDirect). Anything that prints to a socket printer works: POS software, CUPS, mobile apps, ERP label printing.

Default ports: 80 web app and REST API, 9100 raw print, 5353 mDNS, 631 IPP (Raspberry Pi build).
